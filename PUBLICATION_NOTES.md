# Sources, interpretation and public scope

## Provenance

The Office artefacts were downloaded from the team's shared OneDrive folder on 4 November 2026. The screenshot gallery was supplied as `Team10_Evidence_English_Clean.zip`. Copied source files are byte-preserved; `MANIFEST.json` records their SHA-256 hashes. Text previews and this index were generated during consolidation. The snapshots represent the files available on that date, not independent certification of their claims.

## Stage and evidence boundaries

The browser AR guide is a transition prototype. Rokid HUD guidance remains the intended product direction. A rendered browser scene, automated browser check, screenshot or exported demonstration JSON does not prove a deployed glasses application, real positioning, hands-free benefit, measured campus accuracy or a participant usability result.

The English screenshot package states that interface text was translated in a local rendering without changing the online application. Its member-responsibility captions come from the team presentation; a GitHub account's commits alone do not verify individual contributions. Its result/export captures are an automated demonstration without human participants or subjective ratings. The original package README is retained with the gallery.

Week 8 planning documents are drafts for team review. A team-reported completed pitch deck is distinct from direct file inspection or submission receipts. This repository does not claim that LMO/DC submission has occurred.

## Public scope

Weekly administration/supervisor logs are not republished. Participant registers, consent entries and participant-level Week 5 narratives remain private because the research plan describes team-only storage. Week 5 has an aggregate public summary; Week 6 contains the existing analytical synthesis. No participant identification keys or raw recordings are included. No passwords, OAuth tokens, environment files, account sessions or full chat archives are included.

No course slide decks or assessment PDFs are redistributed. Plans may refer to coursework requirements or external research; links and original attribution should be consulted. No blanket open-source licence is applied to participant research, third-party materials or screenshots.

`EXCLUDED_FROM_PUBLIC.json` lists omitted source categories so the public index does not imply that it contains all private research records.
