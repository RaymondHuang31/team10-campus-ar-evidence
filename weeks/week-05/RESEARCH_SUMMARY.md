Source: the team's supplied Week 5 session reports and Interim Pattern Summary v2, reviewed in the shared-folder snapshot of 4 November 2026. This is a public derivative summary, not an independent verification or a substitute for original contemporaneous notes.

- The team reports three user sessions and one staff session. Two user sessions included a comparison between a phone map and phone-held in-view cues. They were not Rokid-glasses tests.
- The map-check/look-away counts in the two comparison reports were 9 → 4 and 11 → 2. These related actions are not identical measurements, the conditions were not fully matched, and the sample is too small for a general performance claim.
- Fewer look-aways did not consistently improve effort, confidence or preference. Retain the unchanged ratings, no-preference case and recorded cue failures when interpreting the results.
- Existing maps, physical signs, help from others and familiarity remain real alternatives. The staff account does not establish buying authority, willingness to pay or an approved pilot.
- Original notes, researcher-role mapping and route-comparability details were still incomplete in the supplied synthesis. Do not infer that those gaps have since been closed.

Participant-level narratives, consent entries and registers remain in team-only storage. No participant names, contact keys, recordings or signed forms are distributed here. Displayed web-demo session metrics in the screenshot gallery are a separate automated demonstration, not additional participants.
