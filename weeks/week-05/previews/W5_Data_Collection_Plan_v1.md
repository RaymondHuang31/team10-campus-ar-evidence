What we will collect this week

Rules we will follow

Real target users only - not friends standing in (Week 5 deck, slide 3).

Every participant gets an anonymised ID (P-01...) in the register; no real names anywhere in the Evidence Hub.

Consent is read before each session; participation and audio recording are asked separately.

Participants must be 18+; anyone under 18 is excluded at screening.

Raw files (recording / transcript / notes) are stored in the W5 folder and linked from the register the same day.

Contrary evidence is kept, not dropped.

What we will bring to the checkpoint

Research progress log (every session attempt, including no-shows).

Participant register with anonymised IDs, method, date, relationship tag and consent status.

Raw evidence files, indexed by participant ID and dated.

Interim pattern summary, hedged, with contrary evidence retained.
