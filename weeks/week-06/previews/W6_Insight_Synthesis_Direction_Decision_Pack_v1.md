| Item | Statement / change | Evidence | Interpretation | Status | Next check |
| Before: Week 2 problem hypothesis | New students and visitors on unfamiliar university campuses can experience difficulty translating screen-based or fragmented indoor/outdoor navigation information into the real physical environment, increasing wayfinding effort, uncertainty and reliance on repeated map checking or human directions. | Week 2 Problem Statement, section 3. Baseline carried into the Week 4 research plan. | Broad starting hypothesis covering indoor/outdoor navigation. | Existing baseline | Keep the before wording visible for comparison. |
| After: Week 6 narrowed statement | For new students and first-time visitors on unfamiliar campus routes, the priority problem to investigate is deciding which way to walk at junctions and building entrances when a map does not directly match the scene. Existing signs and familiar routes often work. An alternative should demonstrate clearer decisions and useful user benefit before the team commits to a campus-wide AR implementation. | EV-011 to EV-013. The problem severity, frequency and generalisability remain provisional. | Narrows the location and task. Separates the navigation problem from the hardware choice. | Proposed revision | Team and supervisor review. |
| What changed | From fragmented navigation information across the campus to orientation at unfamiliar decision points. From assumed lower effort to checking effort, confidence, clarity and preference separately. | EV-011 observations 5-7, 12; EV-012 and EV-013 measure tables. | Fewer checks alone do not demonstrate lower effort or overall preference. | Evidence-led narrowing | Retest on comparable routes and retain negative cases. |
| Prototype stage | A rough browser-based 3D demo supports destination selection, map/in-view guidance options, HUD/spatial-arrow presentation and first/third-person views. A free-roam interaction and map view were checked. The end-session screen offers browser metrics, feedback and an anonymous-record download button. Unity modelling has not been done. See EV-015. | User clarification on 21 Oct 2026 and the provided demo URL. | An interface/concept prototype is available for feedback. The About page states no GPS, Rokid SDK or glasses connection. Browser metrics are not real-campus position, eye tracking or wearer experience. | Browser demo checked; participant feedback pending | Test destination findability, cue understanding, map confusion and feedback flow before further implementation. |
| What would weaken the direction | Unfamiliar users find existing maps/signs sufficient, or clearer cues do not produce useful improvement in understanding, effort or preference. A better sign or phone map may resolve the issue at lower complexity. | Contrary findings in EV-011 to EV-014. | Keep non-AR alternatives in the concept comparison. | Open assumption | Use findings to retain, narrow or change the direction. |
