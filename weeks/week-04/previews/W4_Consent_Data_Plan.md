1. Consent script - students and visitors

"Hi, I'm a student at XJTLU working on a team project about how people find their way around this campus. I'd like to ask about a recent journey where you had to find a place you hadn't been to before, and in a second part walk one short route with you twice - once with a normal phone map and once with a rough prototype guide. It takes about 25-30 minutes and we'd be walking, so you can stop at any point. Your answers won't affect your studies or your relationship with the university. I'd like to take written notes so I don't misquote you. We'll keep them in a team folder that only the team and our project supervisor can see, your name won't appear in anything we write, and we'll delete the recordings at the end of the module. You can skip any question or stop at any time, and you can ask us to delete your data afterwards. First question: is it okay to take part? [pause for an answer] Second, separate question: do you also agree to an audio recording? You can take part without being recorded - just say no to this one."

2. Consent script - campus staff (separate variant)

"Hi, I'm a student at XJTLU working on a team project about campus wayfinding. I'd like to ask how wayfinding is currently supported and what a small pilot would involve. This is a university research conversation, not a sales meeting, and nothing you say commits you or your department to anything. It takes about 20 minutes. My notes stay in our team folder, shared only with our project supervisor; your name won't appear in our reports, and you can decline any question or stop at any time. First question: is it okay to take part? [pause] Second, separate question: may I record the audio as well? Taking part does not require agreeing to the recording."

3. Outdoor and walking-session safety rules

Choose a low-traffic route and a time that avoids peak movement between buildings.

The participant walks and looks at the guide; the researcher carries the notes and watches for traffic, doors, stairs and cyclists.

Never walk on roads or in vehicle areas. Stop the session if the participant is uncomfortable, distracted or in a hurry.

If AR glasses are used at any point, stop immediately if the participant reports discomfort, dizziness or eye strain, and record it.

Do not photograph or record bystanders. If a photo of the route is needed, take it when nobody is identifiable in frame.

5. Storage, access and retention

Storage: the team's shared OneDrive evidence folder, protected by the team's XJTLU account and password.

Access: team members and the Project Supervisor only. No public or open sharing links.

Pseudonymisation (not full anonymisation): participants are recorded as P1, P2, P3... Because an ID key linking a code to a person exists, the data is pseudonymised rather than anonymous. The key is one separate file inside the team folder, accessible only to the team and the supervisor, and it is deleted together with the recordings.

Retention: recordings and the ID key are deleted by module close (Semester 2, Week 10) unless a participant asks for earlier deletion.

Quotes: any quote used in the log, the pitch deck or the video is attributed to a participant ID and role only.

Withdrawal: a participant can withdraw at any time during a session, and can ask for their data to be deleted up to the Week 10 checkpoint (module close). After that date the recordings and the ID key have already been deleted, so no further action is possible. The participant log records the request and the deletion date.

8. Age and guardianship (under-18 participants)

Participants in this study must be 18 or older. The target groups - university students, adult visitors and staff - are normally 18+, but campus tours and open days can include school-age visitors, so age is checked at screening.

Screen first (S3): if the person is under 18, do not interview them, and record the exclusion in the participant log.

If a minor must be included: obtain written guardian consent before any data collection, plus the young person's own agreement to take part; store both at the same level of protection as the ID key.

Recording a minor: do not audio-record unless the guardian separately consents to recording as well as participation.

Supervisor confirmation: no session involving a participant under 18 takes place before the Project Supervisor has confirmed the arrangement.

Recommended for the Week 5 window: state explicitly that under-18 participants are out of scope, and skip this process.
